Retire the coach from the umbrella; point at its new home

Bob's Ledger now lives at https://github.com/mharrell/bobs-ledger with its
own history, release channel (telemetry/ moved with it) and first-run
launcher. What remains here is the shared pattern and the sync/worktree
tooling, for the next game.

Removed here: hearth-coach/ (the code, meta DB, tests, analysis notes and
img_cache — 1,450 tracked files), telemetry/ (the collector that serves the
coach's releases and telemetry), publish_release.py, the launcher, the icon,
and the six coach-specific agent skills.

WHY, beyond tidiness: a player arriving at this repo could not tell what the
coach was. The umbrella README pitched "AI-assisted game coaches" and the
shipped docs carried 34 references to language models, while the coach calls
no model during play — it advises from a local value function and a curated
meta DB. Framing that describes something the product is not confuses exactly
the people the product is for.

**This repo's history still contains the personal data** that was scrubbed
from the coach's: the maintainer's BattleTag, an opponent's handle, local
profile paths and per-session directory names, all of it from research notes.
Extracting the coach rewrote every one of its commits, so the coach's history
scans clean (verified: 1,291 text blobs, zero findings); this repo's does
not. Treat it as an archive — use the coach repo for anything coach-shaped.
